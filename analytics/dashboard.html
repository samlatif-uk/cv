Unique visitors are daily estimates based on a salted hash of IP address and browser information. No raw IPs or cookies are stored. Visitor-days sum daily uniques, so a returning visitor can count on multiple days. Shared networks and VPNs affect counts and country estimates. Visits reset after 30 minutes inactive or at UTC midnight. Older views have no visitor or location data. 90-day retention; blockers and privacy preferences can reduce counts.
