AI setup & how forecasts work

Manual forecasts and outcome tracking work now. AI generation requires a server-side provider key, a compatible model and explicit enablement. The key is never entered in this page.

On the VPS, edit /etc/sam-trading.env using sudoedit and add:

OPENAI_API_KEY=your-key
INTELLIGENCE_MODEL=your-compatible-model-id
INTELLIGENCE_ENABLED=1
INTELLIGENCE_DAILY_JOBS=10

Then run sudo systemctl restart sam-trading. Restarting pauses the trading bot. Use a model that supports Responses, web search and structured outputs. Set your provider budget before enabling.

Each AI forecast uses one web-research request and three separately prompted forecasts from the same model. The displayed result is their equal-weight mean; the spread is disagreement, not a confidence interval. Research links are citations returned by the provider, not independently fact-checked sources. Each refinement uses one request. Jobs are user-triggered, capped daily and never retried automatically.

Submitted questions, resolution rules and research are sent to OpenAI when you run an AI job. Market odds are manually entered snapshots and are not sent to the forecasting model. Odds gaps are arithmetic comparisons, not proven trading opportunities. Forecasts never place orders or alter your trading strategy.
